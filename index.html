About Me

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematical and Computational Sciences in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Iowa. My academic training is in mathematics, with research interests centered on applied mathematics, mathematical biology, computational modeling, data science, and bioinformatics.

My research interests include developing and analyzing mathematical models for biological and epidemiological systems and using computational and data-driven approaches to investigate real-world problems.

Alongside research, I have extensive experience teaching undergraduate mathematics, including engineering calculus, quantitative reasoning, college algebra, and basic algebra.
